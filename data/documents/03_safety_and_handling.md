# Product Safety and Handling — Synthetic Demo
IMPORTANT: These are fictional demo guidance statements, not certified instructions. The real product label and manufacturer documentation take precedence. Do not make unsupported safety or certification claims.

## General rules
- Follow the product label, included manual, and age grading.
- Inspect products before use and stop using an item that is cracked, broken, frayed, leaking, or otherwise damaged.
- Keep packaging, bags, and small components away from young children where they may create a hazard.
- Adult supervision is recommended for children's products as specified in the catalogue.
- Never claim that a product is "100% safe", "non-toxic", "food grade", "BPA-free", "certified", or compliant with a particular standard unless a current, product-specific evidence record explicitly supports the claim.
- If a user reports injury or possible exposure, advise stopping use and contacting local emergency services or an appropriate professional when urgent; do not diagnose or speculate.

## Children's books and craft items
- Check the age recommendation before use.
- Adult supervision is recommended for activities involving scissors, glue, small craft parts, or tools.
- Use colouring and craft materials according to their own labels.
- Discard or repair damaged pages and bindings; do not let a child chew paper or loose parts.
- The demo records do not establish that any craft accessory is included unless a product variant explicitly lists it.

## Children's cycle
- An appropriately fitted helmet and adult supervision are recommended.
- An adult should inspect brakes, tyres, steering, reflectors, and fasteners before each ride.
- Do not ride in traffic or on terrain unsuitable for the child's skill level.
- Product fit must be checked against the actual manufacturer sizing guide. The demo's 5–7 age range is not sufficient to determine exact fit.
- Do not ride if brakes or steering are faulty or if a component is loose.
- No claim is made that the cycle meets a specific national or international certification standard.

## Wooden Shape Sorter
- Inspect wooden pieces for splinters, cracks, loose parts, or damaged coating before play.
- Wipe clean as directed; do not soak.
- Remove from use if a piece becomes damaged.
- The dataset does not contain lab results for coating composition, choking-hazard testing, or certification.

## Copper bottles
- Follow the care card and product label supplied with the exact bottle.
- Do not assume that all liquids are suitable for copper contact. Do not use acidic beverages unless the product-specific instructions explicitly permit them.
- Do not microwave or freeze the bottle.
- Stop using a bottle if its body, lid, or seal is damaged or if leakage occurs.
- Do not claim that copper bottles cure disease, improve immunity, or provide a medical benefit.
- The dataset does not contain independent lab results for metal composition, migration testing, or potable-water certification.

## Incident handling
- Capture only necessary details: product name/SKU, order reference if available, a short description, and whether immediate danger exists.
- Do not ask the user to upload sensitive personal documents.
- For urgent danger, direct the user to emergency services rather than continuing a routine product conversation.
