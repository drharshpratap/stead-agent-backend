# Customer Service Policies — Synthetic Demo
IMPORTANT: All policies below are fictional test rules, not legal advice or actual Neosh policies. They exist to test question answering.

## Delivery
- Standard dispatch target: within 2 business days after payment confirmation for in-stock items.
- Standard delivery estimate: 3–7 business days after dispatch for serviceable PIN codes.
- Remote-area estimate: 5–10 business days after dispatch, subject to carrier availability.
- These are estimates, not guarantees. Carrier delays, weather, strikes, holidays, address issues, and serviceability can affect delivery.
- Same-day delivery is not offered in this demo policy.
- Dispatch time and transit time are different. Do not add them together unless explaining the stages.
- A delivery estimate cannot be confirmed without the destination PIN code, order status, and current carrier data.
- A product being listed in the catalogue does not mean it is currently in stock.
- If asked for live tracking, say the demo dataset has no live carrier tracking integration.

## Returns and exchanges
- A customer may request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery for an eligible unused item in its original packaging.
- Books with writing, torn pages, missing pages caused after delivery, or signs of use are not eligible unless a verified manufacturing defect is reported.
- Personalised, clearance, and hygiene-sensitive items are excluded from change-of-mind returns in this demo policy.
- Copper bottles must be empty, clean, safely packed, and returned with all supplied accessories.
- A return request is not the same as an approved return. Approval follows order and item inspection.
- Exchanges depend on replacement stock. No exchange is confirmed until inventory is checked.
- Wrong-item or damaged-on-arrival reports should be submitted within 48 hours of delivery with order details and photos where possible.
- Refund processing begins after the returned item passes inspection. Allow 5–7 business days after approval for the demo payment workflow; bank posting time may vary.
- Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is due to a confirmed Neosh fulfilment error or verified defect in this demo policy.
- Items bought through a marketplace must follow the marketplace order's return process unless a written order-specific instruction says otherwise.
- Never promise an exception unless a recorded supervisor approval is supplied.

## Warranty
- Warranty coverage varies by product and is listed in the product catalogue.
- Warranty applies only to verified manufacturing defects within the stated period and with proof of purchase.
- Warranty does not cover ordinary wear, accidental damage, misuse, unauthorised modification, incorrect assembly, or failure to follow care instructions.
- Warranty evaluation may require photographs, SKU, purchase date, and order number.
- Do not guarantee repair or replacement before inspection.
- A warranty period starts from the delivery date in this demo policy.
- If the relevant product-specific warranty is missing, do not apply another product's warranty by analogy.

## Payments
- Demo payment methods: UPI, eligible debit/credit cards, and net banking through the checkout provider.
- Cash on delivery is not enabled in this demo dataset.
- A payment marked pending is not proof of successful payment.
- Never ask users to provide full card numbers, CVV, OTPs, passwords, or banking PINs in chat or voice.
- Payment disputes require an order reference and must be routed to the support process; the demo dataset does not connect to a payment processor.

## Order changes and cancellation
- Cancellation can be requested before dispatch; the system must verify the current order status first.
- A dispatched order cannot be represented as cancelled without carrier/returns confirmation.
- Address changes are only possible before the fulfilment label is created and require verification.
- This dataset has sample order records only and no live order-management connection.

## Customer support
- Support hours in this demo: Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 India Standard Time, excluding listed demo holidays.
- Target first response: within one business day; this is a target, not a guarantee.
- Escalate suspected product injury, fire, chemical exposure, or other urgent safety incidents. Do not provide medical diagnosis.
- Never request sensitive payment credentials or unnecessary personal information.
