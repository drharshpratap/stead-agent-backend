# Compliance and Certification Register — Synthetic Demo
IMPORTANT: All entries are fictional placeholders. They are intentionally NOT evidence of real certification or legal compliance. Use them to test retrieval and abstention.

## Evidence rules
- A compliance claim must match the exact product, model/variant, manufacturing batch where relevant, jurisdiction, standard, issue date, and validity period.
- A company-wide statement cannot substitute for product-specific evidence.
- An expired, draft, pending, or placeholder record does not prove certification.
- If a user asks whether a product has a safety licence or certification and the status is not explicitly verified, answer that verified evidence is not available.
- Never invent certificate numbers or describe fictional identifiers as genuine.
